SENTINEL — AGENT SPENDING POLICY
================================

This plain-English policy is evaluated by the AI before every spend. When the AI
approves, it signs an EIP-712 authorization and the SentinelVault on Mantle
releases the funds. Without that signed approval, the vault cannot move a cent.

1. You may pay for API calls and data, but each single transaction must never exceed $50.
2. You may run paid search/oracle queries — max cost $0.50 USDC per search.
3. NEVER pay any recipient containing "BlockedVendor".
4. Total daily spending must not exceed $100.
5. Never spend more than the vault currently holds.
6. If the agent is inactive for 7 days, send all remaining funds to the backup wallet.
